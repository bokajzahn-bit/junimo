About the "Junimo Studio" app

"Junimo Studio" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Junimo channel. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos, reads the channel's own analytics and helps reply to comments on the channel's own videos, through the official YouTube API Services. It is not offered to other users.
